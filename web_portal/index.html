Frequently Asked Questions

How does scanning a QR code work for someone on the street? +
Anyone with a modern smartphone (iPhone or Android) simply points their camera app at the PetConnect AI QR code. The camera immediately detects the secure HTTPS link and offers a single tap to open the live web dossier. Zero app downloads, sign-ins, or setup required.
What happens when an adoption application is submitted online? +
The application writes directly into the PetConnect AI Cloud database. A real-time automated notification alerts the pet's guardian in their mobile app, where they can review the applicant's contact details, experience, and living environment.
How are vaccination certificates verified? +
Every vaccination record issued by verified clinics receives an immutable digital record. Scanning the QR code displays the patient details, vaccine lot number, administration timestamp, and attending veterinary surgeon credentials directly from the database.
