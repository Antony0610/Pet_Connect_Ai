How Smartphone QR Scanning Works

When an emergency occurs or a pet is up for adoption, no rescuer or good samaritan needs to download an app. Any phone camera opens the live dossier instantly.

01
📋

Generate Smart Poster

Pet parents generate a customized Missing Pet Poster or Emergency Dossier QR directly in the PetConnect AI mobile app. It links directly to this live Vercel cloud portal.

02
📸

1-Tap Camera Scan

Any person on the street points their regular iPhone, Samsung, or Android camera at the QR code. The phone displays a direct link to petconnectai.vercel.app.

03
🚀

Immediate Action & Rescue

The web page opens in under 1 second: pulsating beacon, pet photo, verified reward, direct 1-tap "Call Guardian", WhatsApp GPS dispatch, and online sighting submission.

💡 Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the PetConnect AI Web Portal, QR codes, and adoption workflows.

When a QR code is scanned, does it directly open this website? +
Yes, absolutely! Every QR code created by PetConnect AI encodes a direct HTTPS URL pointing to https://petconnectai.vercel.app. Any smartphone (iPhone or Android) camera automatically detects the URL and prompts the user to open the live web page with a single tap. Zero app installation or login is required.
Will adoption listings show all pet details on the website? +
Yes! Clicking any adoption card or scanning an adoption QR code opens /adopt/:id with high-resolution photos, vaccination credentials, health records, behavioral compatibility tags, caregiver notes, direct phone/WhatsApp contact buttons, and an interactive adoption application.
How does the Missing Pet SOS network dispatch alerts? +
When a pet is marked missing, PetConnect AI dispatches instant push notifications to all volunteer rescuers and clinics within a 10 km radius, generates a scannable poster, and publishes a live emergency beacon on this web portal. Anyone who spots the pet can submit live GPS coordinates and photo sightings directly from the webpage.
Is hosting on Vercel really 100% free? +
Yes! By utilizing Vercel's generous free Hobby Tier under petconnectai.vercel.app, there is zero domain registration cost and zero cloud hosting expense, while delivering ultra-fast edge loading across India and globally.
