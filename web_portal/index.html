Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the PetConnect AI Web Portal, QR codes, and adoption workflows.

When a QR code is scanned, does it directly open this website? +
Yes, absolutely! Every QR code created by PetConnect AI encodes a direct HTTPS URL pointing to https://petconnectai.vercel.app. Any smartphone (iPhone or Android) camera automatically detects the URL and prompts the user to open the live web page with a single tap. Zero app installation or login is required.
Will adoption listings show all pet details on the website? +
Yes! Clicking any adoption card or scanning an adoption QR code opens /adopt/:id with live database details: photos, vaccination status, temperament badges, guardian contact, and an interactive adoption application that notifies the owner's mobile app immediately.
How does the Missing Pet SOS network dispatch alerts? +
When a pet is marked missing, PetConnect AI dispatches instant push notifications to volunteer rescuers, generates a scannable poster, and publishes an active emergency banner on this portal. Anyone who spots the pet can submit GPS coordinates and photo sightings directly from the webpage.
How are other QR codes (Emergency Pass, Vaccine Certificates) verified? +
Scanning a pet's Emergency Pass or Smart Collar QR loads /emergency/:id, which retrieves real clinical vitals (blood type, allergies, microchip ID, caregiver emergency phone). Scanning a vaccination QR loads /verify/vaccine/:id, authenticating the vaccine batch and clinic credentials directly from the database.
